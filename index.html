Our Story · Our Purpose · Our Philosophy

MORE THAN FITNESS. A TRANSFORMATION OF LIFE.

P Cube was born in 2022 in Rohtak, a small city with a big vision.

A vision to redefine how people perceive fitness.

True fitness goes much deeper than physical appearance.

We believe fitness is not just about how you look in the mirror, how much you lift, or how many kilometres you run. True fitness goes much deeper than physical appearance. It is about how you feel, how you function, how you think and how you live.

This belief became the foundation of P Cube.

Our Philosophy

The P Cube Approach

The name P Cube represents the three dimensions of complete well-being: Physical. Physiological. Psychological. Three essential pillars that come together to create a healthier, stronger and more fulfilled individual. We call it being P Cube Fit.

01

Physical

Strength, endurance, mobility and physical confidence.

02

Physiological

Better body functioning, recovery, health and overall well-being.

03

Psychological

Mental strength, confidence, discipline and emotional well-being.

Because being fit is not just about looking fit. It's about being fit in every sense.

Our Journey

People always come first.

Our journey started with a simple thought: what if fitness could become more than a routine? What if it could become a lifestyle that transforms not just bodies, but confidence, mindsets and everyday lives?

From our first steps in Rohtak to building a growing fitness brand, our philosophy has remained unchanged — people always come first.

We have continuously worked towards creating spaces where people feel motivated, welcomed and valued. Spaces that bring together world-class fitness infrastructure, meaningful experiences and a community that inspires one another.

Today, P Cube is growing beyond its beginnings, but our purpose remains rooted in the same belief that started it all.

We are not here just to build gyms. We are here to build healthier lives.

The person behind the vision

It started with transforming one life. Mine.

Before P Cube became a brand, it was a personal journey.

I am Devank Nagpal, founder of P Cube Fitness. And my relationship with fitness began long before I ever thought of building a fitness business.

I once weighed 40 kilograms more than I do today.

I know what it feels like to struggle with obesity, to lack confidence and to wish for a version of yourself that you haven't yet become. My own transformation wasn't just about losing 40 kilograms of fat. It was about discovering discipline, building self-belief and experiencing a completely different relationship with myself.

For me, it felt like a rebirth.

The satisfaction I experienced after transforming my own life was something I had never felt before. It made me realise that fitness had the power to change much more than someone's physical appearance. It could change how a person thinks, feels and sees themselves.

That feeling became my biggest motivation.

I started helping my friends and family members with their fitness journeys. Watching them transform, seeing their confidence grow and knowing that I had played a small part in changing their lives gave me an incredible sense of fulfilment.

Something interesting happened along the way.

I realised that the happiness I received from helping someone transform their life was greater than the satisfaction of earning money.

And that's when the idea of P Cube was born.

In 2022, I decided to take something that had personally changed my life and turn it into something that could change thousands of others.

We started in Rohtak, a small city, with a big dream. There was no grand legacy behind us, just a strong belief in our purpose and the determination to build something meaningful.

I didn't want to build just another gym. I wanted to build a brand that put its clients first, understood their journeys and made them feel that they belonged.

As P Cube started growing, we learnt, evolved, took risks and kept raising our standards. Every new location, every member who trusted us and every transformation we witnessed became another reason to keep going.

Today, when I look back, what makes me happiest isn't just how far the brand has come. It's knowing that something which started with my own transformation is now creating opportunities for thousands of others to begin theirs.

My journey with fitness changed my life. P Cube is my way of helping others experience that same change.

— Devank Nagpal Founder, P Cube Fitness

Need to know

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers to help you plan your first visit.

How do I join P Cube Fitness?

Send an enquiry through the form below or contact your preferred club. The team can explain membership plans, visit options and the joining process.

Can I book a visit before joining?

Yes—use the enquiry form to request a club visit. The team will confirm the available date and time with you.

Do all clubs offer the same facilities?

Facilities and classes can vary by location. Select your preferred club and confirm the current amenities with the team before visiting.

Do you offer personal training?

Personal training is one of the training options offered by P Cube Fitness. Contact your selected location to check coach availability and plan details.

How can I ask about Pilates or group classes?

Choose your preferred location in the enquiry form and mention Pilates or group classes in your message. The team can confirm schedules and availability.

How can I enquire about a franchise?

Select “Franchise enquiry” in the form below, and the team can share the next steps and relevant business information.
